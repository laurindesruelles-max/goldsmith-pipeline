### Beschreibung (YouTube)

You've never heard of the company that probably made your sunglasses. Luxottica designs the frames, owns brands like Ray-Ban and Oakley, makes glasses for Chanel and Prada, runs Sunglass Hut and LensCrafters — and in the US even owns a vision insurer. This is how an orphan from Milan built it, how a rival was pushed off the shelves and then bought, and why your glasses cost what they cost.
