## Beschreibung
In 2018, one line in Burberry's annual report revealed it had destroyed £28.6 million of its own unsold products in a single year. It wasn't alone. This is the hidden economy of everything that was never supposed to be sold: why luxury brands burn stock, the US tax rule that can refund duties on destroyed goods, the Amazon warehouse with a weekly "destroy" target, the $890 billion returns machine, and where the leftovers really end up.

Would you rather a brand burn its leftovers, or sell them at a discount? Tell us in the comments.
